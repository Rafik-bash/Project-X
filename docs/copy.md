## 8. FAQ (`#faq`)

**Section eyebrow:** FAQ
**Title:** Good questions.

**Do you bring your own supplies?**
Yes. Every team arrives with eco-certified products, microfiber, and HEPA vacuums. If you'd like us to use something of yours, just leave it out with a note.

**Are you OK with pets?**
Very. Tell us about them when you book. We'll keep doors closed if needed and use products that are safe around paws and noses.

**Do I need to be home?**
No. Most clients leave a key, a code, or a lockbox. Your team texts when they arrive and again when they leave.

**What if I need to cancel or reschedule?**
Free up to 24 hours before your clean. Inside 24 hours there's a $40 fee, because your team has already planned their day around you. *(placeholder)*

**Are you insured?**
Fully. Every team member is background-checked, trained in-house, and covered by our liability insurance. Certificates are available on request.

**How does pricing work?**
You pay by service and home size, plus any extras. You see the total before you confirm and pay only after the clean. No hidden fees, no contracts.

**What if something isn't right?**
Tell us within 24 hours. If we miss a spot, we come back within 48 hours and fix it, free. That's the whole guarantee, and we mean it.
